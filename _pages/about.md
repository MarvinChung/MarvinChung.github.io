## Biography

Chi-Ming Chung is a research assistant at [Academia Sinica](https://www.sinica.edu.tw/en/), currently collaborating with [Yu-Te Wang](https://homepage.citi.sinica.edu.tw/pages/yutewang/index_en.html) on integrating brain signals with robotics. Previously, he worked as a research engineer at [MediaTek Research](https://i.mediatek.com/mediatekresearch) and earned his master's degree from National Taiwan University under the supervision of [Winston Hsu](https://winstonhsu.info/). His research focuses on real-time NeRF-SLAM systems, with a current emphasis on developing real-world robotics and brain-computer interface (BCI) systems.
